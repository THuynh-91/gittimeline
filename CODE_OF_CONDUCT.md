# Code of Conduct

GitDance is a creative, technical, open project. We want it to be a place where maintainers, contributors, artists and learners can collaborate without fear.

## Our standards

- Be kind and direct. Critique work, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming intent.
- Respect that repository history belongs to real people: performances celebrate collaboration, they do not rank or shame contributors.
- No harassment, discrimination, doxxing or sustained disruption, in any project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact channels; reports are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
